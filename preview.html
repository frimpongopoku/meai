Winter is here, and so are the heating bills. But here's the good news: there are real ways to stay warm, save money, and cut your carbon footprint — all at the same time. This month, we're focusing on the actions that make the biggest difference when the temperature drops.

No-cost Home Energy Assessment

Before you do anything else, start here. A free home energy assessment from Mass Save will show you exactly where your home is losing heat — and what you can get for free or heavily subsidized to fix it. You'll get instant energy-saving products like LED bulbs shipped to your door, plus access to rebates that cover up to 100% of air sealing and insulation costs.

Schedule your free assessment at masssave.com or call Mass Save to book your appointment.

Insulate and Air Seal Your Home

If your home was built before 1970, you're likely heating the outdoors along with your living room. Proper insulation and air sealing can dramatically cut your heating bills and make your home more comfortable. Mass Save covers up to 100% of air sealing and insulation costs — but you need that home energy assessment first to qualify.

Get your free home energy assessment, then follow through on the insulation recommendations to lock in those savings.

Learn more →

Green Heating & Cooling with Heat Pumps

Heat pumps are one of the smartest investments you can make for year-round comfort. They heat your home efficiently in winter and cool it in summer, all while slashing your carbon footprint. With generous rebates and financing options available, now is a great time to explore whether a heat pump makes sense for your home — especially if you're replacing an aging system.

Start with a free Mass Save home energy assessment, then get quotes from qualified installers to explore your options and available rebates.
