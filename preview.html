Winter in Medfield means cozy nights — and sometimes eye-watering heating bills. The good news? There are more ways than ever to stay warm, cut costs, and reduce your carbon footprint. Whether you're ready for a heat pump upgrade or just want to seal up drafts, this month's newsletter has simple steps you can take right now.

Insulate and Air Seal Your Home

If you live in an older Medfield home, you could have 250% more air leakage than a modern house. That's cold air sweeping in all winter long. The fix? A free Mass Save Home Energy Assessment gets you started — and Mass Save will cover 75-100% of the cost of approved insulation and air sealing work.

Schedule your no-cost home energy assessment at masssave.com or call 1-866-527-SAVE.

Green Heating & Cooling with Heat Pumps

Heat pumps are one of the most effective ways to cut your carbon footprint and stay comfortable year-round. They heat in winter, cool in summer, and work best in well-insulated homes. Right now, state and federal incentives — including 0% loans through Mass Save — make this the perfect time to upgrade.

Start with a free Mass Save audit, then get quotes from qualified installers at masssave.com.
